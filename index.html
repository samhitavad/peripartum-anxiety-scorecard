Peripartum Anxiety
Risk Scorecard

Five-item screening tool for pregnant mothers in rural India's caregiving communities. A demonstrative tool derived from a case study project.

01 / TRAUMA

During her pregnancy, did the mother experience any form of emotional or physical trauma? (Select all that apply)

02 / FATHER'S HISTORY

Does the father have a history of drinking, smoking, or other recreational intoxicants? (Check all that apply)

03 / CONSANGUINITY

Is the father, in any way, related to the mother? (Consanguinity)

04 / INFECTION

During her pregnancy, did the mother experience a viral or bacterial infection?

05 / GENETIC COUNSELING

Has the parent ever heard of or experienced genetic counseling before?

*Observed prevalence of peripartum anxiety among households in this score band, from a 416-household case study of caregiving families in rural Puducherry and Tamil Nadu (Vadapalli, Clinton Fellowship for Service). This scoring rule was derived from, and validated within, a population of mothers whose children were later identified as having a disability; its accuracy has not yet been independently tested with pregnant mothers more generally. It is offered here for pilot use, not as a validated clinical or diagnostic instrument.

Intended for use with mothers who are currently pregnant or have recently given birth, at antenatal or early-postnatal contact.
